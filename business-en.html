Hydrogen & Carbon-Neutral City

Hydrogen & Carbon-Neutral City Research

We plan hydrogen cities that integrate the full cycle of hydrogen production, storage and use into urban space, and carbon-neutral cities that demonstrate greenhouse gas reductions in measurable terms.

Key services

  • Master plans for hydrogen pilot cities, and planning and comprehensive plans for hydrogen city projects
  • Basic plans and business models for carbon-neutral leading cities
  • Feasibility studies for hydrogen specialized complexes and production bases, and support for local fiscal investment reviews
  • Operating systems, support networks and integrated safety management for hydrogen cities

Related laws & plans

  • Hydrogen Economy Promotion and Hydrogen Safety Management Act
  • Framework Act on Carbon Neutrality and Green Growth
  • Comprehensive Hydrogen City Plan

Energy Transition & GHG Reduction

Energy Transition & GHG Reduction Policy

We research implementation strategies and feasibility to turn the carbon-neutral goals of central and local governments into actionable policies and projects.

Key services

  • Carbon-neutral city strategies and green city competition proposals
  • Feasibility reviews for RE100 communities
  • International standards and simulation tools for GHG reduction
  • Renewable energy project strategies and carbon reduction feasibility

Related laws & plans

  • Framework Act on Carbon Neutrality and Green Growth for Coping with Climate Crisis
  • Energy Act
View 9 Carbon Neutrality projects

Urban & Regional Development

Urban & Regional Development Planning

We build the case for projects through urban and regional development concepts and feasibility studies consistent with higher-level plans.

Key services

  • Feasibility studies for urban development projects and station-area development concepts
  • Preliminary feasibility studies for free trade zone designation
  • Basic plans for smart green industrial complexes
  • Guidelines for rural special districts, and urban–rural co-development strategies with megacities

Related laws & plans

  • National Land Planning and Utilization Act
  • Urban Development Act
  • Act on Rural Spatial Restructuring and Regeneration
View 7 Urban & Regional projects

National & Local R&D

National & Local Government R&D

Through our corporate research institute, we take part in national R&D projects in land, transport and industry to develop institutions and models.

Key services

  • Institutions and models for plant safety cities based on national territory (3 years)
  • System integration and verification technology for hydrogen electric trams (3 years)
  • Planning of the 2050 carbon-neutral city pilot model project
  • R&D planning for intelligent circulation systems of unused resources and energy

Key clients & partners

  • KAIA
  • KIAT
  • KEIT
View 7 National R&D projects

International Development Cooperation

International Development Cooperation (ODA)

We support Korean institutions expanding overseas through policy research and feasibility studies on climate action and infrastructure in developing countries.

Key services

  • Survey of climate-responsive water management policies in ODA priority partner countries
  • Feasibility studies for overseas international airport modernization
  • Overseas expansion strategies for Korean smart cities and green cities

Key clients & partners

  • Export-Import Bank of Korea (EDCF)
  • KOICA
  • World Bank

Our Process

Process
  1. Step 1

    Policy Design

    We review higher-level plans, laws and guidelines, and turn the client’s policy goals into a concrete strategy.

  2. Step 2

    Planning & Feasibility

    We prepare master plans and basic plans, and analyze technical and economic feasibility.

  3. Step 3

    Investment Review

    We prepare supporting materials and business plans for local fiscal investment reviews and competitive programs.

  4. Step 4

    Implementation

    We support design procurement and operating systems so that plans become real projects.
