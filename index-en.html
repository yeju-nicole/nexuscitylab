Nexus City Lab · Hydrogen & Carbon-Neutral City

Turning policy into plans,and plans into real change in cities.

Nexus City Lab is an R&D consulting firm specializing in hydrogen cities, carbon-neutral cities, and the urban planning and policy research that make them happen.

About

Since 2018, we have carried out research and planning with central and local governments and public corporations where cities and energy meet.

From hydrogen pilot city master plans and carbon-neutral leading city plans to national R&D projects, we produce plans and documents that clients can use right away, grounded in laws, guidelines and statistics.

Backed by our corporate research institute and R&D service registration, we support the whole process from policy design to feasibility studies, investment review and design procurement.
