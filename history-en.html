2023 — 2025

Under a new name, Nexus City Lab, we expanded hydrogen and carbon-neutral city planning to local governments nationwide.

2025

  1. 02
    Renamed Nexus City Lab Co., Ltd.Formerly KOBIZ Co., Ltd.
  2. Key projects
    • Master plans for the Uljin and Ulsan (Phase 3) hydrogen city projects
    • Business models for carbon-neutral leading cities (Dangjin, Jeju, Nowon, Boryeong)
    • Business plan for the Taean County hydrogen city project
    • Feasibility study for Beira and Pemba international airports, Mozambique

2024

  1. Key projects
    • Uljin hydrogen city planning study and Yangju hydrogen city master plan
    • Pyeongtaek hydrogen specialized complex planning and fiscal feasibility support for a hydrogen production base
    • Guidelines for rural special districts and urban–rural co-development strategies

2023

  1. 03
    Kyungsik Noh appointed CEO
  2. Key projects
    • Comprehensive plan for Pyeongtaek hydrogen city
    • Feasibility review of an RE100 community in Saemangeum Smart Waterfront City
    • Basic plan for the Songsan Green City smart green industrial complex and carbon-neutral city
    • Feasibility studies for the Donghae Port free trade zone and Wonju Munmak urban development

2018 — 2022

We established our research institute and laid the foundation for hydrogen city research with the Ulsan hydrogen pilot city and national R&D.

2022

  1. Key projects
    • Carbon-neutral city strategy study for the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport
    • Operating systems and support networks for hydrogen pilot cities
    • Gwangmyeong carbon-neutral green city competition
    • Planning of the 2050 carbon-neutral city pilot model project (national R&D)

2021

  1. Key projects
    • Hydrogen pilot city master plan for Ulsan Metropolitan City
    • Basic study on hydrogen city development for LH
    • Launched national R&D on plant safety cities and hydrogen electric trams
