Turning policy into plans,and plans into real change in cities.

Nexus City Lab is an R&D consulting firm specializing in hydrogen cities, carbon-neutral cities, and the urban planning and policy research that make them happen. Since our founding in 2018, we have worked with central government ministries, local governments, LH and local public corporations, and public research institutes on hydrogen pilot city master plans, carbon-neutral leading city plans and national R&D projects, wherever cities and energy meet.

Responding to the climate crisis and achieving carbon neutrality are no longer matters of declaration but of implementation. For good policy to lead to real change in cities, it takes plans aligned with higher-level frameworks, project structures that can secure funding, and well-grounded documents that clients can put to use immediately. We focus on closing that gap with rigorous analysis based on laws, guidelines and statistics, and twenty years of accumulated policy research experience.

From hydrogen cities that integrate the full hydrogen cycle into urban space, to carbon-neutral cities that prove emission reductions in numbers, to regional development where cities and rural areas grow together, and overseas expansion through international development cooperation, Nexus City Lab supports the entire process as one continuous flow: from policy design to feasibility studies, investment reviews and design procurement.

We will continue to work alongside our clients with reliable research and dedicated advice, so that their policy goals become feasible plans and projects. Thank you for your interest in and support of our company.
